About the Collection

Purpose

I created this curated collection to make reliable information about losing body fat easier to find and understand. With so much conflicting information available online, it can be difficult to determine what is useful and what is not. The purpose of this curated collection is to provide useful information on the journey to losing body fat, but not doing so in a one size fits all way.

Scope

This project does not list all of the proven methods for losing body fat. It does, however, provide a baseline for anyone getting started. This project is not an ultimate guide to losing body fat, and it is important to find what works for you and to be consistent.

How to Use this Collection

Users can explore the collection according to their interests and priorities. Resources are organized into categories including Fat Loss Overview, Nutrition, Definitions, Exercise, and Additional Resources, making it easier to locate the information you need. Each resource also has a brief annotation detailing what it covers, why it's valuable, who may benefit, and any limitations it may have. Clicking "View Source" on any of the sources will take you directly to that source in a new tab.

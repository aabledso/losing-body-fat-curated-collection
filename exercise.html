Research Article 02

Effect of Exercise Training on Fat Loss - Energetic Perspectives and the Role of Improved Adipose Tissue Function and Body Fat Distribution

Kristopher Jensen Kolnes, Maria Houbrog Petersen, Teodor Lien-Iversen, Kurt Hojlund, and Jorgen Jensen

Research

Source: Frontiers Physiology: The Effect of Exercise Training on Fat Loss – Energetic Perspectives and the Role of Improved Adipose Tissue Function and Body Fat Distribution

What it covers: This article covers past studies that have been performed to explain relationships contributing to weight loss, such as the impact of exercise on AT function and adipocyte cell size.

Why it's valuable: This source is valuable because it references many other useful resources and explains exactly what’s happening in our bodies when we are exercising and how that translates to losing body fat.

Who would benefit: Users who want scientifically backed information and those who want to use exercise as their main driver during their fat loss journey will benefit the most from the information provided in this article.

Limitations: The article doesn’t have many instances where it discusses experiments done with high-intensity training, which is what most people usually aim for when going to the gym nowadays.

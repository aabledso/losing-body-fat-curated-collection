Tool 01

TDEE Caculator Website

Maple Tech

Tools Intake

Source: Maple Tech. (2019). TDEE calculator. Calculator.Net.

What it covers: This calculator tool provides an energy requirement estimate based on users’ weight, height, and activity levels.

Why it's valuable: This calculator tool is valuable because it provides a general idea of users’ maintenance calories and BMI, which can then be used to find the intake required for a deficit.

Who would benefit: People who are unsure how many calories they should be consuming each day can start here. They will have a better understanding of what their calorie goals should be.

Limitations: This calculator uses the Katch-McArdle formula, but does not account for all of the factors that go into everyone’s daily lives so it is important to use the tool only to get a general idea of your intake.

Research Article 02

Physiological Process of Fat Loss

Salwa Refat El-Zayat, Hiba Sibaii, and Karima A. El-Shamy

Enhancers

Source: El-Zayat, S. R., Sibaii, H., & El-Shamy, K. A. (2019). Physiological process of fat loss. Bulletin of the National Research Centre, 43(1).

What it covers: This article covers supplements, enhancers, and many other contributors to fat loss.

Why it's valuable: This source is valuable because it provides a scientific explanation of how fat-loss occurs in our bodies. It is also useful for comon weight loss claims with research-based information.

Who would benefit: Users who find value in knowledge will benefit the most from the information provided in this article. Those who enjoy reading and understanding how their body will react to different supplements, training methods, diets, and enhancers will also benefit.

Limitations: This article is from 2019 and some information is outdated and does not include information that’s become increasingly popular in the community: peptides.

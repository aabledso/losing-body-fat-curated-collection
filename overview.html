Web Article 01

Steps for Losing Weight

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

Beginners Setting Goals

Source: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. (2024, February 26). Steps for losing weight. Healthy Weight and Growth. https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/losing-weight/index.html

What it covers: This article by the CDC covers five steps that people should take when trying to lose weight.

Why it's valuable: The CDC is a government organization that is respected by many people, making it a reliable source. The steps are practical, simple, and able to be tailored to fit an individual’s lifestyle.

Who would benefit: People with busy lifestyles who are interested in losing weight/body fat would benefit because the steps are very straightforward yet still allow room for customization.

Limitations: Steps aren’t specific. For example, it suggests tracking what you eat and your sleep, but doesn’t specify the nutrients you should aim for or the recommended hours of sleep one should strive to achieve.

Web Article 03

Why a Calorie is Not a Calorie?

Krunoslav, fitness trainer

Calories

Source: krunoslav. (2020, December 27). Why a calorie is not a calorie? Bodybuilding Wizard.

What it covers: This article emphasizes the importance of focusing on the types of calories we consume while also challenging the idea that “a calorie is a calorie.”

Why it's valuable: I’ve heard people say a calorie is a calorie before and even believed it myself when I first started my journey. This article is valuable because Krunoslav explains the why behind the importance of focusing not just on how many calories you’re consuming but also how those foods will affect your body and energy levels.

Who would benefit: Anyone who believes “a calorie is just a calorie” will benefit from the information provided in this article. People who are just getting into calorie counting will also benefit.

Limitations: This article makes a few overgeneralized claims.
